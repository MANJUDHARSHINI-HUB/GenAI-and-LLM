Character Profile
Ryomen Sukuna is the primary antagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen. Known as the "King of Curses", he was the most powerful jujutsu sorcerer during the Heian Era — the Golden Age of Jujutsu. His power was so feared that even the authorities of Japan held festivities in his honor rather than oppose him.
👁 Heian Era ⚔️ King of Curses 🔥 Fire & Slash
Legendary Abilities
Sukuna possesses the most transcendent cursed energy in history, surpassing even special-grade sorcerers. His innate technique Shrine combines slashing attacks (Dismantle & Cleave) with devastating fire-based powers. He mastered Domain Expansion: Malevolent Shrine, which warps space and shreds everything nearby without a barrier.

Physical Prowess
Beyond his cursed techniques, Sukuna is a master hand-to-hand combatant with immense strength, speed, and durability. He can punch through skyscrapers, outrun opponents at 60+ km/h, and tank attacks like Gojo's Hollow Purple. His true form wields four arms and two mouths, allowing him to cast complex techniques while fighting.
Vessel & Legacy
Sukuna's essence was divided into 20 indestructible fingers, each containing cursed energy equivalent to a special-grade curse. He currently inhabits Yuji Itadori as his vessel, though he later transferred to Megumi Fushiguro's body using the Ten Shadows Technique. Even Gojo Satoru acknowledged that a fight against Sukuna would be incredibly difficult.
